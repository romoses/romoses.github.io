Receipts

A private tool that keeps a complete, auditable record of business expenses.

Receipts is a personal command-line tool. It pairs every business expense on a bank statement with the receipt for it, so the full record can be handed to an accountant or the Australian Taxation Office on request.

What it does

  1. Reads receipt emails from a dedicated Gmail mailbox and files the attachments into a folder in the owner's own Google Drive.
  2. Reads the date, amount and merchant from each receipt.
  3. Absorbs bank transaction exports (CSV) the owner has downloaded from their bank.
  4. Matches receipts to bank charges, and reports the charges that still have no receipt.

Who it is for

One person — the author. It is not a product, there is no sign-up, no hosted service and no other users. It runs on the author's own machine, against the author's own Google account.

Your data

Everything it touches stays in the owner's own Google account, with one exception: receipt images and PDFs are sent to Anthropic's API to read the date and amount off them. This is set out in full in the privacy policy.
